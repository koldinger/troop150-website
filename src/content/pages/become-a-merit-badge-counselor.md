# Steps to becoming a merit badge counselor.

- Register as an adult with the troop, if you haven’t already.
  - [Boys (150)](https://my.scouting.org/VES/OnlineReg/1.0.0/?tu=UF-MB-492taa0150)
  - [Girls (5150)](https://my.scouting.org/VES/OnlineReg/1.0.0/?tu=UF-MB-492taa5150)
- Take [SYT (Safeguarding Youth Training)](https://training.scouting.org/learning-plans/2025), if you haven’t already:
  - Available on [My Scouting](https://my.scouting.org), on the right side of the page on the "S.A.F.E Guarding Youth" icon, if the above link is out of date.
- Register with the council.   This is a no cost registration, as you’re already registered with the troop.
  - Get the fillable [PDF registration form](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/pdf/524-501.pdf).   You can fill it out online and download the completed version.   Don’t worry about the “To be filled in by unit”.
- Download the completed version and email it to: <492.membership@scouting.org>.  Be sure to indicate that you want to be a merit badge counselor.
- Wait a couple of days, and check on [My Scouting](https://my.scouting.org/tools/my-applications).    You should see multiple registrations, including one that says "Merit Badge Counesor",
- Go to [Badger](https://cpcscouting.org/login-to-badger/), and create an account.
- Login to [Badger](https://cpcscouting.org/login-to-badger/).   Click on the “Merit Badges” Icon (red, with a hammer and wrench) and scroll down to “Submit a request”.   Pick as many merit badges as you like and submit each one.
  -  Be sure to include a reason why you’re qualified.   Can really be as simple as “I like [whatever the merit badge is about]” but more info is useful.
  -  Indicate if you only want to be a counselor for the troop, the district, or the whole council.
- You may get a confirmation email.   Or check back in Badger.   It's usually approved within a business day or so.
- Counsel Scouts.
